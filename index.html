03 / Research direction

What can we trust
when agents act?

At Greenwich, my doctoral research investigates security and trust in agentic AI. Chagu provides a continuing space for prototypes, questions, and experiments.

Current research directions

01 Evidence & provenance +

How can we trace the sources, actions, and dependencies behind an agent’s behavior across system boundaries?

02 Behavior & anomalies +

How can statistical and learned signals help identify unusual behavior in complex, interacting systems?

03 Accountability & recovery +

How can evidence support a proportionate response—and help us assess whether a system has recovered?
