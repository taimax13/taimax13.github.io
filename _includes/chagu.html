ChaGu – The Secure AI Guardian

ChaGu (ChainGuard) is a blockchain-backed, agentic security protocol designed to defend modern AI infrastructure. From anomaly detection to prompt-level protection, ChaGu is built to think before it acts—and act when it must.

Key Capabilities:

  • Agentic AI Firewall: Monitors prompt inputs, inspects behaviors, and actively intervenes against suspicious or adversarial usage.
  • Blockchain Trust Layer: Ensures auditability and traceability in data transformations and AI outputs.
  • RAG-Enhanced Security: Uses retrieval-augmented generation and pattern detection to assess context risk in real time.
  • Machine Whisperer Mode: Detects micro-signals in model behavior before failures or threats escalate.

ChaGu has evolved through hackathons, real-world simulations, and critical post-mortems. It's a living system designed to scale with AI's growing complexity.
