I’m an AI engineer and researcher experienced across cybersecurity, infrastructure, and intelligent automation.

Connecting ideas to systems.

My work includes model development and evaluation, anomaly detection, LLM agents and workflows, MLOps, and distributed infrastructure. I approach these through the behavior of the whole system: what it does, how it is observed, and what happens when it fails.

As a PhD Researcher at the University of Greenwich, I investigate security and trust in agentic AI. I created Chagu, a continuing protocol development and research initiative, and SARAD, an open-source anomaly detection framework for synthetic aperture radar imagery.

I was selected as one of 200 participants in the 2024 GenAI Pioneer Program and participate in the Nebius Academy AI Performance Engineering Fellowship.

I work with international teams on AI engineering and cybersecurity, combining research with practical delivery.
